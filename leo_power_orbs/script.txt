Format     1080x1920 vertical, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC 48 kHz stereo
Length     90.00 s = exactly 2700 video frames = exactly 4,320,000 audio samples
           (checked with ffprobe after muxing; re-muxed if off by even one frame)
Beats      44 beats. Times are fixed and never move.

HOW VO LINES ARE FITTED
  Each line is generated as its own MP3 and its silence is trimmed, then it is measured.
  Every line must sit inside its VO WINDOW (beat start +0.10 s ... beat end -0.05 s).
  1. Fits                -> placed as is.
  2. Up to 15% too long  -> sped up (pitch-preserving), at most 1.15x.
  3. Still too long      -> replaced with the pre-approved SHORT line listed below it.
  Lines marked (!) will probably need step 2 or 3.

LEGEND
  VO   = voice-over (Leo narrating)
  SFX  = sound effect @ start time. Every SFX also gets an on-screen [BRACKET] caption.
  MUS  = theme-song state. FULL = 100 %, BED = ducked to ~20 %.

SOUND DESIGN NOTES
  Rarity dings escalate:  small (16.05) -> medium (36.05) -> dramatic (52.10) -> epic (66.00)
    growing in length (~0.6 s / 1.2 s / 2.5 s / 4 s), weight (more low end each time),
    reverb size, and level (+0 / +2 / +4 / +6 dB relative to the first).
  Music moods:  hook = theme, intro = sleepy, purple = playful, gold = heroic,
                black = spooky, rainbow = epic, outro = theme ending.
  Mix:  VO loudest > SFX medium > music low (BED ~20 %).
        Final master: about -14 LUFS integrated, true peak <= -1 dBTP.

CAPTION PLAN  (1080x1920; avoid the bottom 20 % (y > 1536) and right edge (x > 920))
  VO captions   Inter Black, white, thick black outline, 2–3 words per chunk, each word
                pops in (scale 125 % -> 100 %) as it is spoken, using ElevenLabs character
                timestamps. Centered at x≈500, y≈1180–1400.
  SFX captions  [IN BRACKETS], Inter Black Italic, about 55 % of VO size, cyan with a dark
                outline, same pop animation, upper band y≈260–340. They stack when sounds overlap.
  Rarity words in captions and labels use the orb's color:
    PURPLE / COMMON / SUPER SPEED       -> purple  #B347FF
    GOLD / DECENT / SUPER STRENGTH      -> gold    #FFC61A
    BLACK / RARE / SHADOW POWERS        -> black text with a white outline
    RAINBOW / EXTREMELY RARE            -> rainbow gradient
  Rarity labels and the title card sit in the band y≈420–780.
