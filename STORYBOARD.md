# Storyboard / script: "My cousin heard me call 911..."

A 2:00 vertical (1080x1920, 30 fps) Roblox-style horror story. All visuals, voices, music and SFX are generated by the code in `src/`. The characters and sets are original blocky designs (no Roblox assets or logos). Timestamps are `m:ss.ss`. Voice timings are the measured lengths of the generated TTS.

Captions pop in word by word, synced to the voice. Key words are shown in red.
